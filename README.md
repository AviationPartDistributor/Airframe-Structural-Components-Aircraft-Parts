## Understanding Aircraft Airframe Structural Components

Aircraft structures consist of numerous interconnected components that work together to provide the required strength and support. Primary and secondary structural elements can differ significantly in their design, material, installation method, and aircraft application. Frames, stringers, longerons, bulkheads, skins, beams, brackets, fittings, and reinforcement components are examples of structural elements that may be encountered during aircraft maintenance.

Structural components can be manufactured from aluminum alloys, titanium, steel, composite materials, and other specialized aerospace materials. The selection of a particular material can depend on factors such as strength, weight, corrosion resistance, temperature exposure, fatigue characteristics, and the location of the component within the aircraft structure.
